Privacy

The Book of Nines · last changed 8 November 2026

Your book stays on your device. There is no account, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising. Only when you play with friends does a little leave your device: the name you gave your book, its player ID, and your moves in that match.

Who is responsible

The Book of Nines is made by Alexander Tea (Tea Story) in Switzerland. Questions or requests about your data: alexanderteastory@proton.me, or WhatsApp and Telegram at +41 76 558 88 31.

What stays on your device

Your progress, coins, stickers, book styles, settings, the pages you make or receive, your match history and any match you keep for later are saved in your browser's storage on this device. We never receive them.

A save code (the Binder's Record) is made on your device and goes only where you paste or send it. The same is true of page codes and their QR codes. A page code carries the page's title, the grid, the date it was made, the name of the book that made it and that book's player ID.

When you play with friends

For a match, the game sends the other players and, for a match with a room code, the multiplayer server:

  • the name you give your book (choose any name; it need not be your real one),
  • your book's player ID, a random code made on your device when you name your book,
  • the room code, and your moves: boxes completed, sheets solved, or giving up.

The other players in the room see your book's name, its player ID and your progress in the match. Nobody else does.

The Google Play app and the website use our multiplayer server for matches with a room code. In the app it runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., under Cloudflare's privacy policy); data may be processed in Cloudflare's data centres worldwide. It keeps a room only as long as the match needs it: an unstarted room for 3 days, a match for 14 days after its last move, a finished match for 3 days. Then it is deleted. To deliver the data, the server also sees your internet (IP) address. It uses the address only to stop one address from opening too many rooms: it is kept in memory, scrambled (hashed), for at most 10 minutes, and never written down. Our hosting provider may keep its own technical logs under its own policy.

The version on claude.ai runs matches through Claude's own room feature, under Anthropic's privacy policy.

Direct play over Wi-Fi connects the two devices to each other. To find the way, each device asks a public address service run by Google (stun.l.google.com), which sees your IP address.

We use this data only to run the match you asked for (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)) and to protect the service from abuse (Article 6(1)(f)).

Camera

The camera is used only when you press Scan, to read a QR code. Images stay on your device and are neither stored nor sent.

Fonts

The app and the website carry their own typefaces, so no font service is contacted. Only the version on claude.ai loads them from Google Fonts, as claude.ai provides.

Links and advertising

The WhatsApp and Telegram buttons on the credits page open those apps; nothing is sent until you send a message there, and their own terms apply. The television shows no real advertising. If that changes, this page will say so first, and you will be asked for consent where the law requires it.

Children

The game asks for no personal details. We suggest that young players give their book a nickname rather than their full name.

Your rights

You can see, change or delete everything kept on your device yourself: rename your book in the game, or clear the app's or website's data to remove it all. Match data on our server deletes itself as described above; ask us if you want it removed sooner or want to know what is held. You may also complain to a data protection authority: in Switzerland the FDPIC (Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner), in the EU the authority of your country.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
